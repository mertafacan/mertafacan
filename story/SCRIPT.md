# The Prize That Vanished on a Shelf

True story: **George de Hevesy and the Nobel medals** (Copenhagen, 1940). Voice: Kokoro (free, CPU), voice `am_michael`. **185 words, 59.0 s voice, 59.4 s video, 1080x1920, 30 fps.**

> The quoted joke in line 3 is a dramatisation. Facts: Hevesy dissolved the Laue and Franck gold medals in aqua regia, left the beaker on a lab shelf while soldiers searched the institute, and recovered the gold after the war so the medals could be recast.
